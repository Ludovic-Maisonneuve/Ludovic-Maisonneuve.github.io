Evolutionary biology · mathematical models

Ludovic Maisonneuve

Research fellow, Institute for Advanced Study in Toulouse

I am an evolutionary biologist and research fellow at the Institute for Advanced Study in Toulouse. I use mathematical models to study the ecological, social, and life-history determinants of the evolution of behavior and its consequences. During my PhD, I first focused on the evolution of criteria used to choose mates. I now study what explains the diversity of social information-use strategies observed in nature and their consequences for how individuals adapt to their environment.
